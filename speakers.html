Innovator in Residence Spring 2026 · AI & Music Production

The Speakers

It was the goal of the residency to be an honest exploration of AI in music (and the arts in general), showing both the powerful potential and the very real negatives. To help us out, we brought in working experts in various fields related to AI — including lawyers, environmentalists, actors, creators, and technologists — to speak about how AI is reshaping their corners of the creative world. Every one of these conversations helped us form educated opinions and practice on how to use AI in an ethical, legal manner, or to choose to not use it at all.
